The Slice

A creature that lives in four dimensions: three of space and one more, w. Our world is the 3D slice at w = 0, so we only ever see where its body crosses that slice. As it drifts and turns along w, it grows from nothing, changes shape, splits and vanishes, like the sphere visiting Flatland (Abbott, 1884).
